**Event:** Rutgers Health Hackathon

**Problem:** Ureteral injuries are a serious complication in abdominal and pelvic surgeries. Real-time identification of ureter location during surgery can prevent inadvertent damage.

**Solution:** Developed a computer vision system to detect and highlight ureteral structures in laparoscopic video feeds, providing real-time anatomical guidance to surgeons.

**Approach:**
- Object detection on laparoscopic surgical video frames
- Transfer learning from pretrained medical imaging models
- Real-time inference pipeline for intraoperative use

**Skills:** Python, PyTorch, OpenCV, medical image analysis
